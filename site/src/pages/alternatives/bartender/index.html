A free Bartender alternative for Mac: Coat Check

Bartender is the best-known menu bar manager, and it does a lot. If what you need is to reach the icons the notch hides, Coat Check does that for free, and it’s open source.

Updated October 19, 2026

The short version

Choose Bartender if you want full control of your menu bar: hide icons on a schedule or a trigger, search them, change spacing, and keep profiles for different setups. It costs $24.99 once for version 7, or $19.99 a year for Bartender Pro.

Choose Coat Check if your problem is simpler: icons disappear behind the notch and you want them back without reorganizing anything. Click the hanger, see every icon, and open the one you need, including its own menu. It’s free and MIT licensed.

Why some people left Bartender

In 2024 Bartender’s original developer sold the app to a new owner, Applause, without telling customers. People noticed when the app’s signing certificate changed and an analytics framework appeared in an update. The developer later apologized. Menu bar managers often hold Accessibility and Screen Recording permissions, so many users moved to open-source apps they could inspect.

Coat Check asks only for Accessibility, which it needs to list other apps’ icons and click them. It never asks for Screen Recording, and every line is on GitHub.

What works differently

  • Bartender moves icons; Coat Check doesn’t. Bartender hides icons by managing your menu bar layout. Coat Check leaves your layout alone and adds one list of everything.
  • Coat Check shows an app’s real menu. For apps that expose their menu, you choose the item right from Coat Check’s list, without first revealing a hidden bar.
  • Bartender does far more. Triggers, search, spacing, and profiles have no equivalent in Coat Check, by design.

Using both

Coat Check doesn’t move or hide anything unless you turn on its divider, so it can run alongside Bartender. Use Bartender to arrange and hide icons, and Coat Check to open any icon from one list.
