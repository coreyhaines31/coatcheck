A Vanilla alternative for notched MacBooks

Vanilla hides menu bar icons behind a pair of dots. Its own help page notes that on a MacBook with a notch, macOS can hide the dots too. Here’s how Coat Check compares.

Updated October 19, 2026

The short version

Vanilla, by Matthew Palmer, is a minimal icon hider. Click its dots to hide or show icons to their left. It’s free, and Vanilla Pro ($10 once) adds a hotkey, auto-hide after 5 seconds, and a section for icons you never want to see.

On a notched MacBook, the icons Vanilla reveals still have to fit to the right of the notch, and Vanilla’s help page says its own dots can end up hidden too. Coat Check shows every icon in a list under its hanger instead, and opens any of them from there.

What works differently

  • Vanilla clears space; Coat Check reaches what’s hidden.
  • Coat Check shows each app’s menu, so you can choose an item without revealing anything.
  • Coat Check is open source. Vanilla is closed source.

Using both

Coat Check doesn’t move or hide anything unless you turn on its divider, so it can run alongside Vanilla. Use Vanilla to arrange and hide icons, and Coat Check to open any icon from one list.
