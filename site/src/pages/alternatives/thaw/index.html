Thaw vs Coat Check: two free Mac menu bar apps

Thaw is the actively maintained fork of Ice: free, open source, and full of features. Coat Check is a smaller tool for one job. Here’s how to choose.

Updated October 19, 2026

The short version

Thaw picks up where Ice left off. It hides the icons you choose, shows them in the Thaw Bar under the menu bar, and adds groups, spacers, profiles per display or Focus, and a long list of triggers. It releases often: Thaw 2 for macOS 26, and Thaw 3 in alpha for macOS 27.

Coat Check doesn’t reorganize your menu bar. Click the hanger to see every icon, with the ones the notch hides first, and open any of them, including its own menu.

What works differently

  • Thaw moves icons to hide them. It needs Accessibility to move menu bar items, and it may restart some apps while rearranging. Coat Check never moves an icon.
  • Coat Check opens an app’s own menu. You choose the item from Coat Check’s list, without opening the Thaw Bar or anything else first.
  • Thaw is the bigger toolbox. If you want triggers and profiles, Thaw has them and Coat Check doesn’t.

Using both

Coat Check doesn’t move or hide anything unless you turn on its divider, so it can run alongside Thaw. Use Thaw to arrange and hide icons, and Coat Check to open any icon from one list.
