The MacBook notch is hiding your menu bar icons

On a MacBook with a notch, menu bar icons only fit to the right of the camera. When they run out of room, macOS hides the extras. Here’s how to tell, how to make room, and how to reach the rest.

Updated October 19, 2026 · macOS 14 Sonoma to macOS 27

Which Macs have a notch

Every 14-inch and 16-inch MacBook Pro since 2021, and every MacBook Air since the M2 in 2022, including the 15-inch models. The 13-inch M2 MacBook Pro has no notch. Apple calls these screens Liquid Retina (MacBook Air) and Liquid Retina XDR (MacBook Pro).

What happens to icons that don’t fit

Menu bar icons line up from the right edge of the screen toward the notch. App menus (File, Edit, View) fill in from the left. Once the icons reach the notch, there’s nowhere left for them to go.

macOS 14 to 26The icons that don’t fit simply aren’t shown. There’s no overflow menu, no arrow, and no sign anything is missing. The app is still running; you just can’t click its icon. Apple’s own support page says menu bar items can “appear blocked or hidden behind the camera housing.” On a Mac without a notch the same thing can happen when an app’s menus are long enough to reach the icons.

macOS 27macOS now folds icons that don’t fit behind a small overflow indicator at the edge of the menu bar. Click it and the hidden icons expand to the left of the notch, over the app menus. Click again to put them away. You can’t choose which icons go behind it.

How to tell if an icon is hidden

If an app is running (it shows in Activity Monitor, or opening it again does nothing visible) but its icon is nowhere in the menu bar, the notch is the likely cause. A quick test: quit an app whose icon you can see, and watch whether a missing icon appears in its place.

Make more room in the menu bar

Remove icons you don’t use

Hold ⌘ and drag an icon off the menu bar. Turn off Apple’s items you don’t need, like Spotlight or Siri, in System Settings. On macOS 26 Tahoe and later, you can also switch off other apps’ icons under System Settings > Menu Bar > Allow in the Menu Bar. See all the menu bar settings.

Put the icons you use most on the right

Icons closest to the right edge are the last to be hidden. Hold ⌘ and drag the ones you rely on toward the clock.

Tighten the spacing between icons

macOS has two hidden settings for the gap around each icon. Smaller values fit more icons. In Terminal, run:

defaults -currentHost write -globalDomain NSStatusItemSpacing -int 12
defaults -currentHost write -globalDomain NSStatusItemSelectionPadding -int 8

Then log out and back in. To undo it, run:

defaults -currentHost delete -globalDomain NSStatusItemSpacing
defaults -currentHost delete -globalDomain NSStatusItemSelectionPadding

and log out again. These aren’t official settings. They work on macOS 14 to 26, and there are early reports that they stopped working on macOS 27.

Use a “More Space” resolution

In System Settings > Displays, choose a resolution labeled More Space. Everything gets smaller, so more icons fit, at the cost of smaller text.

Apps that put their menus behind the notch

Some older apps don’t know about the notch and put menus where the camera is. Apple’s fix: quit the app, select it in Finder, choose File > Get Info, and turn on Scale to fit below built-in camera. The app then runs in a shorter area below the notch. The option disappears once the developer updates the app.
